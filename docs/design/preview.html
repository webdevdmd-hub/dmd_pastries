Pastries POS — design system v2

Monochrome by default. Color means money.

Neutrals carry zero chroma, so the only saturated thing on any screen is the thing that moves money or warns you. Two densities over one token set: the counter gets 48px targets and a mono readout, the ledger gets 44px rows and tabular figures. The total is the memorable thing — readable across a counter, tied to the ledger.

01 / counter

Point of sale

Every target is at least 48×48px, including the variants button that is h-7 (28px) today. Cards lift on hover instead of changing border color. Payment method is a segmented control, not two competing buttons. One green button on the screen, and it is the one that takes the money.

03 / color

Achromatic base, one accent

04 / type

Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif

Geist is a modern grotesk with a matched mono, which solves the money problem in one family instead of pairing two unrelated ones. Negative tracking on everything above 14px, scaling to −0.045em on the total. No uppercase, no letterspacing, no weight above 600 — those three are the dated tells in the current build, where font-black appears 78 times.

Pastries

What v1 got wrong

  • Cream #FBFAF8 canvas, warm chroma in every neutral
  • Uppercase labels with 0.12em tracking
  • A tan accent at 3.2:1 that could never carry text
  • A visible border on every card
  • Cormorant Garamond, a 2016 display serif

What v2 does

  • Zero-chroma neutrals, exactly shadcn's ramp
  • Sentence case, negative tracking, no letterspacing
  • One accent, reserved for money and success
  • bg-muted fills instead of borders
  • Geist + Geist Mono, one family, matched widths

05 / controls

Buttons and density

--radius: 0.625rem, shadcn's default, down from the current 0.875rem. 36px in the ledger, 48px at the counter. Focus is always the blue ring, never the brand — a brand ring on a brand button is what the current build has, and it is invisible.

06 / threshold

Where the bakery shows up

Login, receipt header, marketing. Identity lives in one serif wordmark and a lot of space, not in a tinted background. This replaces the two 18rem blurred gradient orbs drifting on an 11-second loop behind the current login screen.
